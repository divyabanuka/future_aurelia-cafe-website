## 💡 Business Problem

Local businesses can benefit from having a professional website where customers can easily find information about the business.

The website provides a single place for customers to:

- Discover the café
- Explore the menu
- View photos
- Check opening hours
- Find the location
- Contact the business
- Learn about the café

---

## 🚀 Business Benefits

The website can help a café:

- Improve its online presence
- Build customer trust
- Attract potential customers
- Showcase products and services
- Make communication easier
- Provide location information
- Provide opening-hour information
- Create a professional brand image

---

## 🗣️ Business Pitch

Aurelia Café & Bistro is designed to give a local café a professional online presence.

Customers can quickly explore the menu, learn about the café, view food and café images, read customer reviews, check opening hours, find the location using Google Maps, and contact the business through phone or WhatsApp.

The website is simple, attractive, responsive, and easy to use on mobile devices, helping customers discover and connect with the café.

Objective:
Create a professional website for a local business and demonstrate how an online presence can help the business improve visibility, build trust, and make it easier for customers to connect.
